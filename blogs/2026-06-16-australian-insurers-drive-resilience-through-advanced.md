Australian insurers are modernizing natural catastrophe claims handling as extreme weather events cost roughly $4.5 billion annually. Drawing on the response to Tropical Cyclone Alfred in 2025, the piece shows the shift from reactive to preventative approaches using predictive modeling, drone technology, and Guidewire ClaimCenter to speed claim resolution.
