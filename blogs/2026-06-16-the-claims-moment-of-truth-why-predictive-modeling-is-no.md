Predictive modeling in claims management identifies high-risk files early, arguing that what a bodily injury claim finally costs depends more on the path it takes after FNOL than on how it looks at intake. As experienced adjusters retire and workforce gaps widen, the piece presents predictive modeling as essential for capturing institutional knowledge and improving outcomes across entire portfolios.
